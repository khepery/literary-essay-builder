✨ Introduction Paragraph

Your introduction has 3 parts: Hook, Background, and Thesis Statement. 你的引言有3个部分：开篇钩子、背景信息和论点陈述。

🪝 Part 1: Hook (75-125 words)

Capture the reader's attention with a compelling opening. Use a thought-provoking question, surprising fact, vivid scene, or relevant quote. 用引人入胜的开头吸引读者的注意力。使用发人深省的问题、令人惊讶的事实、生动的场景或相关引言。

🎯 Part 3: Thesis Statement (35-60 words)

Your thesis = Observation + Analysis + Significance. It should be arguable and provide a roadmap for your essay. 你的论点 = 观察 + 分析 + 意义。它应该是可论证的，并为你的论文提供路线图。

🎯 Conclusion

Wrap up your essay (150-250 words). Restate thesis and reflect on significance. 总结你的论文（150-250字）。重述论点并反思意义。

📋 Conclusion Structure

  • Restate your thesis in new words用新的词语重述你的论点
  • Summarize your main arguments briefly简要总结你的主要论点
  • Extend the significance—why does this matter?延伸意义——这为什么重要？
  • End with a thought-provoking final statement以发人深省的结束语收尾
