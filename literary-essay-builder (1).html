✨ Introduction Paragraph

Your introduction has 3 parts: Hook, Background, and Thesis Statement. 你的引言有3个部分：开篇钩子、背景信息和论点陈述。

Your thesis should be arguable—that is, something that you think is true about the text, but which is not a simple matter of fact. It must be complex enough to develop through evidence and arguments across the course of your essay. 你的论点应该是可论证的——也就是说，你认为关于文本的真实内容，但不是简单的事实。它必须足够复杂，以便通过证据和论点在整篇文章中发展。

📐 Thesis Formula | 论点公式

Thesis = Observation + Analysis + Significance
论点 = 观察 + 分析 + 意义

  • Observation: Usually regarding the form or structure of the literature. This can be a pattern, like recurring literary devices. Example: "I noticed the poems use symbols such as the lover's longing" 观察：通常关于文学的形式或结构。这可以是一种模式，如重复出现的文学手法。例如："我注意到诗歌使用了恋人渴望等符号"
  • Analysis: You could also call this an opinion. This explains what you think your observations show or mean. "I think these recurring symbols all represent the human soul's desire." This is where your debatable argument appears. 分析：你也可以称之为观点。这解释了你认为你的观察显示或意味着什么。"我认为这些重复出现的符号都代表人类灵魂的渴望。"这是你可辩论的论点出现的地方。
  • Significance: This explains what the significance or relevance of the interpretation might be. Why should readers care? "I think these recurring symbols all show the human soul's desire to connect with God." This is where your argument gets more specific. 意义：这解释了解释的重要性或相关性可能是什么。为什么读者应该关心？"我认为这些重复出现的符号都显示了人类灵魂与上帝连接的渴望。"这是你的论点变得更具体的地方。

⚖️ What is the difference? | 有什么区别？

❌ Weak Thesis:

"The works of ecstatic love poets Rumi, Hafiz, and Kabir use symbols such as a lover's longing and the Tavern of Ruin to illustrate the human soul's desire to connect with God."

较弱的论点：简单陈述事实

✅ Strong Thesis:

"The ecstatic love poets Rumi, Hafiz, and Kabir consistently employ symbols such as the lover's longing and the Tavern of Ruin to reveal a shared pattern: the soul's pursuit of divine union, a pursuit that both elevates personal experience and challenges conventional religious authority."

强有力的论点：具体、可辩论、有意义

Notice: The strong thesis makes specific, arguable claims (highlighted). It's not just describing—it's arguing a complex interpretation. 注意：强有力的论点提出了具体的、可辩论的主张（已突出显示）。它不仅仅是描述——而是论证一个复杂的解释。

🪝 Part 1: Hook (75-125 words)

Capture the reader's attention with a compelling opening. Use a thought-provoking question, surprising fact, vivid scene, or relevant quote. 用引人入胜的开头吸引读者的注意力。使用发人深省的问题、令人惊讶的事实、生动的场景或相关引言。

🎯 Conclusion

Wrap up your essay (150-250 words). Restate thesis and reflect on significance. 总结你的论文（150-250字）。重述论点并反思意义。

📋 Conclusion Structure

  • Restate your thesis in new words用新的词语重述你的论点
  • Summarize your main arguments briefly简要总结你的主要论点
  • Extend the significance—why does this matter?延伸意义——这为什么重要？
